What We Do

Three ways we serve your case

  • Expert Opinion Letters

    Professionally drafted evaluations submitted in support of asylum claims, refugee cases, humanitarian parole applications, and SAID petitions — grounded in direct adjudicative experience.

  • Mock Asylum Interviews

    Realistic practice interviews that prepare applicants for the questions, pacing, and expectations of USCIS officers — reducing anxiety and strengthening credibility presentation.

  • Program Consultation

    Advisory support for NGOs, law firms, and humanitarian programs navigating policy complexities, case strategy, or staff training on immigration adjudication standards.

No AI on Your Case

Asylum declarations and humanitarian testimonies contain some of the most sensitive details a person can share — trauma histories, persecution accounts, personal disclosures. At Candor Evaluations, every case is read and drafted by a person. We do not route your documents through AI writing tools, automated summarization, or language-model processing of any kind. Your client's story deserves a human reader.

About the Founder

Expertise built inside the system that decides these cases.

Candor Evaluations was founded by a former U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services officer with direct adjudicative experience in asylum and humanitarian matters. That background means our evaluations are written by someone who has sat on the other side of the desk — someone who understands what decision-makers look for, what credibility gaps trip up otherwise strong cases, and how to frame an expert opinion that actually moves the needle.
