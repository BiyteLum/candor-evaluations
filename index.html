Our Standard

No AI on
your case. Ever.

Asylum declarations contain some of the most sensitive details a person can share — trauma histories, persecution accounts, disclosures that took courage to put on paper. They deserve a human reader who takes that seriously, not a language model that processes them as tokens.

At Candor Evaluations, we don't use AI writing tools, automated summarization, or any form of machine processing on case materials. That's not a limitation — it's the service.

What we do — and don't do
Every case read by the evaluator personally
Letters drafted from scratch by an expert
Documents deleted on request
AI summarization of declarations
Automated letter generation
Third-party processing of case materials
